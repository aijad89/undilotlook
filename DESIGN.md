# Design System: undilot

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Service Counter"**

undilot reads like a government service and works like a well-made dashboard: a navy shell that holds still, white work surfaces on a cool grey ground, one blue that acts. The office sits at a desk for hours scanning draw lists, so its screens are dense, Stripe-calm tables and forms at 40px controls. Usahawan, many older and on a phone, get the same world at a larger size: 1rem body, 48px targets, one large button per draw. Every screen, office, portal and public alike, sits on the same 1000px column down the middle.

Nothing is decorative. Depth is a hairline and a whisper of shadow; colour carries meaning (blue acts, gold marks what is yours, state colours always name themselves in words). Two moments are authored: the draw-stage stepper's current stop settling in, and the lot reveal a usahawan watches on confirming a lot. The system refuses the grey dashboard with a single accent and the frosted Apple imitation it replaced.

The whole thing ships with **no build step**: every stylesheet is an inline `<style>` in a Blade partial, and the only libraries (Bootstrap 5.3.3, Bootstrap Icons 1.11.3) come from a CDN with pinned versions and SRI hashes. Tokens live once, as `--ui-*` custom properties in `partials/ui-tokens.blade.php`; nothing else spells a hex value except the two places CSS variables cannot reach (see The No Stray Hex Rule).

**Key Characteristics:**
- Navy shell (rail, participant bar, sign-in panel), white surfaces on a cool grey ground.
- One 1000px column on every page; one top bar and one footer shared by the portal and the public pages.
- One action blue; Kraftangan gold only for "yours", "you are here" and the mark.
- GOV.UK yellow-and-black focus ring on every focusable thing.
- System UI type with tabular figures on every number.
- Hairline borders, 12px card corners, 8px control corners.
- The draw-stage stepper on every draw, office and portal alike.
- Two component prefixes that never meet: `.mac-` for the office, `.ap-` for usahawan.

## Colors

A cool, official palette: navy for the frame, white for work, one saturated blue for action, and a single warm gold held back for ownership.

### Primary
- **Action Blue** (`blue`): every primary button, filter button, checked checkbox, active tab underline, the stepper's done and current stops, link text (`blue-text`). Hover deepens to `blue-hover`, press to `blue-press`; `blue-tint` is the quiet background for info alerts, active text-size toggles, row-action hover, an unread notification, an info row on the admin dashboard and the signed-out Sign In button's hover. `blue-tint-2` is the deeper tint; `blue-border` (row-action hover, unread notification), `blue-border-2` (portal outline button) and `info-border` (info alert) are its hairlines; `row-hover` is the near-white blue wash on an admin table row. `selection` is the text-selection wash.

### Secondary
- **Kraftangan Gold** (`gold`, `gold-tint`, `gold-text`): the usahawan's own lot (the lot link on a draw card, the lot code on the lot page, the reveal's seal and code), the active-nav marker (a 3px bar in the admin rail, an inset bar in the participant drawer, an underline in the participant bar, the active nav icon), and the brand-mark tile on every shell, the footer and the sign-in panel. Text on gold is always `gold-text` on `gold-tint`, never white on gold. `gold-tint-2` is the lot link's hover.

### Tertiary
- **Service Navy** (`navy-950` to `navy-700`): the shell. `navy-900` is the admin rail, the participant bar and drawer, the sign-in panel, the active pagination page, the month band of a date tile and the split-flap tiles of the lot reveal; `navy-700` is the active nav item fill; `navy-950` is ink on gold. Text on navy is `on-navy`, secondary `on-navy-2`; the nav uses four more greys on navy: `on-navy-3` for item text, `on-navy-soft` for submenu items, `on-navy-icon` for resting icons, `on-navy-dim` for the drawer's section label and the rail's chevrons. Pure white on navy stays the literal `#fff`.

### Neutral
- **Cool Ground** (`ground`): page background under every shell.
- **White Surface** (`surface`) and **Wash** (`surface-2`): cards, inputs, bars and the footer; `surface-2` for table headers, card footers, input-group addons, an event poster without a banner, and the envelope of the lot reveal.
- **Hairlines** (`border`, `border-strong`, `sep`, `border-hover`): `border` outlines cards, bars and the footer's top edge, `border-strong` outlines controls, `sep` divides rows inside a card, `border-hover` is a control's border (and an event card's) under the pointer.
- **Shade** (`shade`): a translucent ink edge that darkens the border of a coloured fill, such as a painted plot tile.
- **Scrim** (`scrim`): `navy-950` at 72%, behind a modal. The mobile nav drawers keep their lighter navy scrims (see Layout).
- **Ink** (`text`, `text-2`, `muted`): headings and values, labels and secondary copy, metadata and placeholders.
- **State pairs** (`ok`, `warn`, `bad`, `neutral`, `violet`, each with `-tint` and `-text`): text on tint, every pair at least 4.5:1. The solid `ok`/`warn`/`bad` are for fills (success button, progress bar, danger hover, badge counts); words use the `-text` shade. Tinted surfaces take a matching hairline: `ok-border`, `warn-border` and `bad-border` for alerts, the stronger `warn-border-2` and `bad-border-2` for warning and danger buttons and the danger row action.

### Named Rules
**The One Blue Rule.** Only `blue` acts. A secondary action is white with a strong hairline; a destructive action is white with `bad-text` until hover turns it solid `bad`. On the navy bar the signed-out Sign In is the one white-filled button, because blue does not read on navy.

**The Gold Means Yours Rule.** Gold marks the usahawan's lot, where you are in the nav, and the mark. It is never a button fill, a status, or a highlight for anything else.

**The No Stray Hex Rule.** Colour comes from `--ui-*` tokens, and no view, partial or script outside `ui-tokens` spells a hex value. There are exactly two exceptions, both because CSS variables cannot reach them, and both name the token each value mirrors: `App\Support\CalendarPalette::PALETTE` (PHP data written into inline styles and event data, one comment per value) and `pdf/lot-result.blade.php` (the PDF renderer reads no CSS variables; a header comment lists the tokens). Change a token, change it there too. The local-only `dev/auto-login` view is out of scope. The legacy `--mac-*`, `--ap-*`, `--adm-*`, `--apple-*` and `--pub-*` names survive only as aliases onto `--ui-*` so old inline styles resolve; new code uses `--ui-*` directly.

## Typography

**Body Font:** Segoe UI Variable Text (with Segoe UI, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, Roboto, Helvetica Neue, Arial, sans-serif)
**Mono Font:** ui-monospace (with Cascadia Mono, Segoe UI Mono, Consolas), for `code` only

**Character:** One system sans at every level, set firm (600 to 700 for headings and labels) with slight negative tracking on headings. It reads as a service, not a brand.

### Hierarchy
Ten sizes and no others: 0.75, 0.8125, 0.875, 0.9375, 1, 1.125, 1.25, 1.5, 1.75 and 2.25rem. Every rem size in the views (outside `pdf/` and `dev/`) is one of them; a new size snaps to the nearest step. The sign-in panel title's `clamp(2rem, 3.4vw, 2.8rem)` is the one fluid size.

- **Display** (700, 2.25rem, 1.15, -0.02em): the public event list's headline and an event page's title (1.5rem on a phone); also the day on a large date tile and large state glyphs (empty-state and done icons).
- **Headline, portal** (700, 1.75rem, 1.2; 1.5rem under 576px): the page title and greeting in the participant portal.
- **Stat** (700, 1.75rem, 1, tabular): portal stat values and the plan reference's counts.
- **Headline, admin** (700, 1.5rem, 1.25; 1.25rem under 768px): the office page title; also the sign-in form heading and the portal identity name.
- **Figure** (700, 1.5rem, tabular): the counts in a draw's band, a lot fact's value, the lot code in the reveal, the day on a date tile, the admin dashboard's totals, and a public section heading (Past events).
- **Small heading** (700, 1.25rem, 1.3): a draw card's title and an event card's title on the public list.
- **Lead / section title** (700, 1.125rem): portal card, section and empty-state titles, a past event's title, brand names, the footer panel's title, the sign-in subtitle, and the lot code chip on the lot page.
- **Title** (650, 1rem): admin card titles, the admin empty-state title, a notification's title, an event's name on the admin dashboard, the portal bar's page title on a phone.
- **Body** (400; 1rem/1.55 in the portal and public pages, 0.875rem in admin tables, fields, alerts and details): portal reading copy, labels and table cells are 1rem; portal secondary copy (breadcrumb, small button, timeline note, footer, stepper labels, an event fact's label) is 0.875rem and nothing in the portal goes smaller except badges and table headers. Footer panel copy caps at 72ch.
- **Note** (400, 0.9375rem, 1.5, `muted`): the one line beside or under an event's call to action, on the public list and an event's page ("Lot draw open until ...").
- **Small** (600 or 500, 0.8125rem): admin meta and secondary copy (breadcrumb, status pill, row action, form label, detail label, pagination, section title, `.mac-muted`, the admin dashboard's sub-heads and split rows), stacked-table cell labels, portal table headers, fact labels and the drawer's section label.
- **Label** (600, 0.75rem, 0.02em): admin table headers, `.mac-badge`, nav count badges and stepper labels, sentence case. The one uppercase text is a date tile's month abbreviation (700, 0.06em), which is a calendar convention, not a heading.

### Named Rules
**The Tabular Figures Rule.** Every table cell, `time`, draw meta line, lot code and count sets `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`, so columns of numbers line up when scanned.

**The Bigger For Usahawan Rule.** The portal sets every equivalent role one step larger than admin, and its text-size toggle sets the root to 1.125rem / 1.25rem (112.5% / 125%). The office gets the same toggle at 112% / 124%.

## Layout

One column, three shells over one token set.

- **The column:** every page's content sits on one 1000px column down the middle (`--ui-page-w`), padded 16px a side (`--ui-page-pad`) until the window is 1032px wide, when the padding drops to 0. A column is `max-width: var(--ui-page-max); margin-inline: auto; padding-inline: var(--ui-page-pad)`. The participant bar's contents and the footer line up on the same column. The scrollbar gutter is always reserved, so centred content never jumps between short and long pages.
- **Office (admin layout):** a fixed 264px navy rail on the left, a sticky 60px white top bar, and content on the grey ground in the same column, padded 24px (so the frame is 1000px plus 48px). Pages run header (breadcrumb above the title, primary action at right), then a filter bar, then a table card. Below 768px the rail becomes a drawer over a navy scrim (`navy-950` at 45%) and the bar drops to 56px. The office has no footer.
- **Portal (frontend layout) and public pages (public layout):** one shared participant bar and one shared footer, so a usahawan walking from the event list to their dashboard never sees either change. The bar is sticky, 64px, navy. At 992px and up the nav (Events, then Dashboard once signed in) sits in the bar beside the brand; below, it moves to a 280px drawer and the bar keeps only menu, page title, text size, notifications and the user (or Sign In). Portal content starts 32px below the bar (20px on a phone); public event pages start 56px below (24px on the list, 16px on an event page, on a phone).
- **Footer:** white, a `border` hairline on top, as tall as the bar (64px), 64px below the content, 0.875rem `muted`. The mark and copyright sit left, the office's footer pages and social links right; under 640px it stacks, centred. A footer page opens in a white panel directly above the footer (copy capped at 72ch and 260px tall, scrolling inside). Both layouts are a full-height column whose main area grows, so on a short page the footer meets the bottom of the window.
- **Two-column pages:** an event's page runs its story beside a 300 to 380px facts card (32px apart); the admin dashboard runs 3fr beside 2fr (16px apart). Both stack under 860px.

Rhythm is 8px: control gaps 8px, card padding 20 to 24px (30px on a public event card), page sections 16 to 24px apart, portal section heads 36px above, public sections 56px above, 12px between event cards. Breakpoints: 576px (portal compact), 640px (public cards and the footer stack), 768px (admin stacks), 860px (two-column pages and sign-in stack), 992px (the participant nav moves into the bar), 1032px (the column loses its side padding).

### Named Rules
**The One Column Rule.** No shell sets its own width. Every page, and the bar and footer that frame it, reads `--ui-page-w`; a new surface takes the column, not a new max-width.

**The Tables Become Cards Rule.** Below 768px every `.mac-table` stacks: each row a bordered 12px card, each cell a label/value line (the value wrapped in `.mac-cell`) whose label is copied from the column header by script. A cell holding one field is labelled like any value; a cell with a button or more than one control, a blank header or a `colspan` is skipped. A wide one-form row (`td[colspan]`) instead shows each field's `aria-label` above it, label and field sharing a `.mac-field` box (the label `aria-hidden`; the box is `display: contents` above 768px), with the row's button on the fields' baseline; a worded button there takes its own full-width line under the fields. Detail rows stack label over value, full width. The outer card gives up its chrome so cards never nest in cards, unless it carries `.mac-card-keep`: a small table inside a detail card keeps the card, and its rows become `sep`-divided lines inside it, labelled at the detail-label size (0.8125rem/500).

**The Say What It Does Rule.** Below 768px an icon-only row button shows its own `title` as a visible word beside the icon (`.mac-btn-word`, added by script, `aria-hidden` so the accessible name still comes from the title), and grows to 40px. Older users should not have to guess what a bare tick or bin does. Above 768px the word is hidden.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat by default, separated by hairlines; shadow is a whisper at rest and only real when something floats or a whole card answers the pointer.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Rest** (`--ui-shadow-1`): cards, filter bar, buttons, event cards and the event page's facts card. Barely there; the border does the work.
- **Raised** (`--ui-shadow-2`): a whole-card link under the pointer (an event card on the public list), and a date tile laid over an event's banner.
- **Pop** (`--ui-shadow-pop`): dropdown menus and the open mobile drawers, the only things that sit above the page.
- **Button bevel**: primary buttons add an inset dark bottom edge over Rest, 1px in the office (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.12)`, 0.14 on the success button) and 2px in the portal and sign-in (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.15)`).

### Named Rules
**The Hairline First Rule.** A surface is separated from the ground by a 1px `border`, not by shadow. Never stack a shadow heavier than Rest on anything that does not float; the one response is Raised on a card that is itself one link, under the pointer.

## Shapes

Gently rounded and consistent, on five steps: 12px (`lg`) for every card, filter bar, alert, dropdown, stacked-table row and event banner; 8px (`md`) for every button, input, nav item, icon tile, date tile and event poster; 6px (`sm`) for row actions, badges, dropdown items, pagination, calendar events and footer social links; 4px (`xs`) for small things: inline `code` chips, colour swatches and calendar dots, small thumbnails, the stepper's track and pips. Status pills, avatars, nav badges, the user button and progress bars are fully round. No other radius: the one exception is the 3px-wide gold active-nav marker, whose 3px (`marker`) corners are sized to its width. The brand mark is a gold square holding a grid icon: 34 to 36px with 8px corners in the shells, 24px with 6px corners in the footer, 12px corners on the sign-in panel. Borders are always 1px, except the 2px stepper ring and the portal's 2px confirm checkbox. The one dashed border is the empty event list.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** gently rounded (8px).
- **Admin:** 40px tall, 0 16px, 0.875rem/600. Primary is solid blue with a bevel; secondary white with `border-strong`; danger and warning are white with a tinted border and state text, filling solid on hover; success is solid `ok`.
- **Portal:** 48px tall, 0 20px, 1rem/600; `-sm` 40px, `-lg` 56px. Adds an outline variant (white, blue text, light-blue border). On a phone the draw card's button is full width.
- **Public:** the event list's action is 240 x 56px at the card's bottom right (full width on a phone); the event page's is full width in its card, 48px. The search button is 48px solid blue beside a 48px input.
- **Row actions (admin):** 32px, 6px corners, 0.8125rem/600, white with `border-strong`; hover tints blue. On a phone an icon-only one shows its title as a word and is 40px tall (see The Say What It Does Rule).
- **On the navy bar:** icon buttons are 44px, `on-navy-3`, a white wash on hover. Signed out, Sign In is a 42px white button with `navy-900` text; a "Quick sign in" outline beside it exists only where the local sign-in shortcut is registered, and hides on a phone.
- **Hover / Focus:** colour transitions at 140ms on the one ease-out curve; focus is the yellow ring. Disabled drops to 45% opacity with no shadow.

### Status

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** take every colour, radius, shadow and duration from `--ui-*` in `partials/ui-tokens.blade.php`.
- **Do** write styles inline in a Blade partial and load libraries from a pinned CDN URL; there is no build step and no Node in production.
- **Do** use `.mac-` classes on office pages and `.ap-` classes on participant pages, and only one of the two on any page.
- **Do** put every page on the one column (`max-width: var(--ui-page-max); margin-inline: auto; padding-inline: var(--ui-page-pad)`), and frame participant and public pages with the shared `portal-bar` and `site-footer` partials.
- **Do** give every focusable element the GOV.UK ring (`0 0 0 3px #ffdd00, 0 0 0 5px #0b0c0c`); plain text links take the yellow highlighter with a black underline instead.
- **Do** show status as a word with its marker, never as colour alone.
- **Do** put the draw-stage stepper on every draw, full on a draw's own page and compact in a table row.
- **Do** keep portal targets at 48px, body text at 1rem, and secondary text (breadcrumb, small buttons, footer, stepper labels) no smaller than 0.875rem.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use gold for anything except the usahawan's lot, the active-nav marker and the mark.
- **Don't** introduce a second action colour; blue is the only one that acts.
- **Don't** put `.mac-` or `.ap-` classes on the sign-in views; they carry their own look.
- **Don't** spell a hex value outside `ui-tokens`; the only exceptions are `CalendarPalette::PALETTE` and the PDF slip, each naming the token it mirrors.
- **Don't** give a shell or page its own max-width; take the column.
- **Don't** add a shadow heavier than Rest to anything that does not float.
- **Don't** mark an unread or active row with a coloured side stripe; use the blue tint and its hairline.
- **Don't** fade a locked tile whose label must stay readable; hatch it.
- **Don't** suppress the focus ring or replace it with a blue glow for keyboard users.
